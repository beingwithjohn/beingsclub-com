## Fair questions

**Eyebrow:** [MINE] Fair questions

**Q1:** [MINE] I've never meditated. Is that a problem?
**A1:** [MINE] No. Instruction is offered to anyone who wants it, and nothing has to be believed — it's there to try in your own experience. Nobody can tell how it went for you, and nobody asks.

**Q2:** [MINE] Do I have to talk?
**A2:** [MINE] Yes — it's a social gathering, and the conversation is the point. Cameras are on and everyone takes part. You don't have to be interesting or arrive with anything worked out; you just have to actually be there.

**Q3:** [MINE] Is it recorded?
**A3:** [MINE] Never. Nothing is recorded and nothing is written up. What's said in the room stays in the room — which is part of why people can be honest in it.

**Q4:** [MINE] Is it religious?
**A4:** [SITE + W-MAY] No. The meditation is an invitation, not a belief to take on — something to experiment with and discover the reality of for yourself. And there is no prescribed way a conversation should unfold.

**Q5:** [MINE] What if I get the same person twice?
**A5:** [W-MAY] Allocation is random, so that can happen — and it is a part of the Beings Club experience. The chance of meeting the same people more than once is a feature, not a fault.

**Q6:** [MINE] What if I can only come sometimes?
**A6:** [MINE] That is how most people come. It is a monthly room, not a course — turn up when it works and leave a gap when it doesn't.

**Q7:** [MINE] How is this different from a Sit?
**A7:** [MINE] A Salon is one evening, monthly, and mostly about meeting each other. A [Sit](/beyondbelief) is a longer commitment for going deeper into practice, with a small group over weeks.
