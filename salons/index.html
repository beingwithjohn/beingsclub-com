Salons · the curiosity social club

Space to connect — to oneself, to each other, and to what is possible.

Every month we gather to take time for ourselves and to meet one another. Every Salon begins with meditation.

Following, members meet either 1:1 or in groups of 3. There are no conversation prompts, there are no themes, and there is nothing to do except stay curious.

Everyone takes part: cameras on, and nothing is recorded.

Nothing to prepare, nothing to have figured out.

The principle

Curiosity connects.

We define curiosity as “an orientation to experience that is open to discovery”.

Practised in this way, we create the conditions for the discovery of connection — to ourselves, to each other, and to what is possible.

Conversations develop in their own way. Each room is a unique combination of experience and intention. Topics, pauses, and new questions arise on their own with curiosity.

Purpose

Regular spaces for connection.

The purpose of Beings Club Salons is to provide regular spaces for connection between individuals, ideas, and aspirations and their realisation.

The intention is that all beings benefit as a result.

Salons are like an online curiosity social club — a room where this happens month by month.

The host

John hosts the Salons.
His role is to support a healthy relationship between known and unknown.

Mutual care

Mutual care is a commitment to reduce harm.

Everybody brings with them a life of depth and richness that we can never fully see into that makes them who they are. People change, and in allowing change we discover our own innate capacity to care.

Salons are designed to support as diverse a pool of people as is possible. Individuals may range in terms of politics, time spent online, religious and spiritual beliefs, and much more.

We are asked to hold each other as precious — each life in the room, including the ones nothing like yours, is treated as real and valuable and worth our care.

If someone reaches out to meet outside of the Salon, please let them know if you can't commit to meeting.

Discovery

Discovery is a process, not an outcome.

We don't know what will happen, we never do, all we can do is show up. Every moment is different and so is every meeting.

Frequently asked questions

I've never meditated. Is that a problem?

No. Whatever your experience, the opening practice is accessible — and nothing has to be believed.

Is it recorded?

No. Salons are ephemeral spaces for those who join them.

Is it religious?

No. John's training is in traditions connected to Jack Kornfield, Tara Brach and David Nichtern, but his appreciation for different traditions is wide, and his own background: multi-faith.

What if I meet the same person twice?

Allocation is random, so it can happen. Meeting someone twice is a feature, not a fault.

What if I can only come sometimes?

That is how most people come. Turn up when it works, leave a gap when it doesn't.

How is this different from Beings Club Sits?

A Salon is one evening a month, mostly about meeting each other. Sits go deeper into practice with a small group over weeks.
