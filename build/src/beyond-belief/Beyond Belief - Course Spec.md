# Beyond Belief
## The Art of Trusting Yourself

**Course specification** — a 35-day experiment, September–October 2026
*Beings Club · Internal · v1, July 2026*

A 35-day experiment on the five principles — curiosity, care, responsibility, play, rest. Each person runs their own experiment, on their own life: they suspect, or already know, that a daily practice might change it. A way into a meditation practice, a deeper sense of trust, and a more embodied life.

## 2 · The premise

It's not always easy to be curious. Sometimes it's easier to defend your point of view, deny something as possible, or simply cast another off as dumb, stupid or weird. It takes more courage to admit you may not know the truth of what is happening — and on the other side of such acts of courage is a kind of liberation. You get freed from who you thought you were, and from the certainty of what you thought must be.

In recognising open space there is much more room, and much more freedom. It doesn't deliver you from the earthly challenges that need navigating, but recognising the openness of how things can unfold makes your approach to them lighter, more optimistic, and often more effective.

The manifestation world hacks at this openness by asking you to stretch your self-concept through affirmation. That can work, to a degree — but affirmations are made to be certainties, and they often feel disingenuous even when they work. **Affirmational work suffers from the unsteadiness of unearned certainty.** The more sustainable path is to recognise the natural, uncontrived, simple existence of spacious openness itself.

**So: nothing here is asked of anyone as belief. Every practice is offered as an experiment, to be tested against experience and discarded if it doesn't hold. That is what the title means.**

## 3 · The five principles

What is trustworthy isn't a set of fixed beliefs. It's a handful of principles — things you can embody and practise. **One holds each week.**

Each principle is introduced live at its gathering, then practised for the following seven days. The week runs gathering to gathering — Wednesday to Wednesday.

| Week | Principle | Held that week |
|---|---|---|
| **One** — 16 Sep | Curiosity | Opening toward what's actually here, and discovering it. Practice partners are paired at each gathering — a different one each week. |
| **Two** — 23 Sep | Care | Gentleness and warmth — turned toward yourself first, which is where most people find it hardest. |
| **Three** — 30 Sep | Responsibility | The ability to respond to what arises — without ignoring what is truly needed. |
| **Four** — 7 Oct | Play | Making it yours — order, length, rhythm, time of day. The week the practice becomes a friend. |
| **Five** — 14 Oct | Rest | Pausing any striving, and finding what is already present, uncontrived and accessible, naturally. |
| **Closing** — 21 Oct | — | Not a new principle. We look back over the thirty-five days together, and end. See §7. |

## 4 · The three aspects of experience

**These are not divided between the weeks.** Body, heart and mind are all present from the first day; the principle changes, the aspect doesn't. Each one has two ways of approach: first you might *cultivate* — a deliberate practice, something to do — then you might *rest*, stop efforting, and let what is already here reveal itself spontaneously.

| Aspect | Cultivate | Rest | Revealed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Body** | Body scans and walking. Turn toward sensation — pleasant, unpleasant and neutral alike. | Drape your awareness over the body. Let its sensitivity become a ground you relax into. | Presence |
| **The Heart** | Loving-kindness. Deliberate warmth, toward yourself and then outward. | Bring awareness to the heart and let its qualities respond on their own, without needing them to any particular way. | Resonance |
| **The Mind** | Note thinking as thinking, and return. Thought becomes one phenomenon among many. | Recognise the mind as naturally spacious. Let phenomena arise and pass by themselves. | Spaciousness |

The heart's reach is not only human. Warmth reaches as readily to an animal, a tree or a place as to a person — towards others and the world, without excluding oneself.

**Nothing to fix. Nothing to change. Nothing to do.**

## 5 · How the practice is held

Two qualities, in balance. **Gentleness** — a softening; treating ourselves and others as precious; a counter to the harshness that can creep in when we apply effort. And **precision** — its opposite and its partner; an exactness, complete contact with reality, a counter to the dullness that settles in when we grow complacent.

Too much gentleness goes slack. Too much precision becomes rigid. When they come into balance, a third thing arrives on its own: **letting go** — not a technique but a result. Right effort, the absence of aggression, the natural release of clinging. You don't do it.

Posture is this same balance in physical form: precision with gentleness. Wakeful and at ease, at the same time — something to play with until it feels right.

## 6 · The daily sit

The Wednesdays are when we meet. **The sit runs for the whole thirty-five days.** Everyone sits every day they can, in their own room — or garden, doorway, park bench — and their own hour — knowing nine other people are doing the same thing that week, with the same principle.

Twenty minutes is standard, and there is no requirement. Sitting daily matters far more than sitting long, and five minutes on a hard day is a real sit, not a failed one. Missed days are expected and built in rather than treated as lapses — beginning again is the practice. No streaks are kept anywhere in the course or the tool.

Outdoor sitting is encouraged rather than merely allowed. And the practice sits better on a natural hinge of the day — first light, the gap before dark — than on a fixed clock hour: those hinges move with the season, and by 21 October they will have moved a long way.

## 7 · The Wednesday gathering

75 minutes, live and online. The shape is John's to set and changes week to week. The only thing to know in advance: we practise together, and explore a new view, quality, approach or theme.

**The sixth gathering — 21 October — is a closing, not a new principle.** We look back over the thirty-five days together; the written companion (handed over at the start) becomes the practice to carry forward; the private line to John closes; and the door to what comes next is opened. A container held, then deliberately released.

Missing one gathering is survivable. Missing three is not — this is a container, and it only holds if you're in it. Nothing is recorded, and nothing said is repeated outside the room.

---

## 8 · Practice partners

Each week you're paired with a different fellow practitioner. Someone sitting the same days, exploring the same principle — to sit alongside and speak plainly with for that week. A companion, not a monitor. How you stay in touch between Wednesdays is yours to decide.

Over the thirty-five days you'll practise with five different people, and meet most of the room. The point isn't one deep bond but the felt sense that the whole cohort is sitting with you — that the container holds you, not any single person.

The inner life was never meant to be walked alone.

## 9 · The practice log

A soft-accountability tool that must disappear into the background: one tap, gone. It makes the diligent feel accompanied and the struggling feel held — never exposed or measured. **No streaks, no scores, no ranking, no guilt.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Daily email** | Optional, sent at a time each participant chooses. A warm invitation with one button — never a mention of streaks, misses, or cohort state. |
| **Log-then-reveal** | The app opens on your own log surface with nobody else on screen. One tap — "I sat" — and only then does the group appear. You join by acting, never measured before acting. |
| **The grid** | Days across, one group of dots per day. Your dot is amber and easy to find; everyone else is just a dot. Amber means only "you sat". A second view shows the whole stretch, one square per day, darker for more of us. |
| **Notes** | One short line after logging, seen by the group. Optional and skippable in one tap. First names only. |
| **Yesterday** | A missed tap can be added the next day, so the record doesn't lie about anyone. |
| **Quiet days** | After four days without a log, John is told privately. The participant is not. Nothing on their screen ever counts or names the gap — they are met with "good to see you", and the tap. |

Full build specification is held separately. Phase 1 is: accounts for ten, self-timed email, deep link to the log surface, one-tap log, log-then-reveal grid, notes, and basic settings.

## 10 · Access to John

For the thirty-five days, every participant has a private line to John — separate from the group, visibly distinct at the moment of writing, and used for honest struggle rather than admin. He answers by voice, usually within a day or two. Where an answer would help others, he re-poses the question generically and answers that — never the person's words, never their name.

**The channel closes at the final gathering on 21 October.** Direct private access returns only through one-to-one work at Space to Be.

## 11 · Who it's for

**This is for you if**
- You've never meditated, or tried an app and it never stuck.
- You'd like to be more open to possibility without having to affirm your way there.
- You want a practice that survives contact with an ordinary, busy life.
- You've noticed you do the things you promise other people you'll do.

**Probably not if**
- You want a quick fix, or a technique that makes difficult feelings go away.
- You're looking for a lineage, a certification, or a final answer.
- You want the Wednesdays but not the thirty-five days.
- These are already the wrong thirty-five days for you.

## 12 · What people leave with

Thirty-five days of daily practice already behind them — not the intention to start, and not a streak they're anxious about breaking. And the written companion — body, heart and mind as places to rest attention, given at the start — which stays useful long after the sit ends.
