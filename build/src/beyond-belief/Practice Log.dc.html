Beings Club Practice Log · the whole experience

Practise. Tap. See the others. Leave.

Everything a build needs: the seven emails, the sixteen mobile screens in the order a person meets them, the four desktop layouts, and the specs underneath — tokens, motion, states, and every line of copy the system can send. The grid is settled: days across, one dot per person, amber is you.

The run is Wednesday 16 September to Tuesday 20 October 2026, with a closing gathering on Wednesday 21 October — so weeks turn on Wednesdays, and so does the grid. Phones drawn at 390pt, desktop at 1152px, emails at 600px single column with no image dependency.

One rule
Nothing before the tap

No cohort, no counts, no notes until you've said you practised. The reward is the room, not a number.

One tap
Everything else optional

A note is offered, never required. The private line to John is always there, never in the path.

Nothing to break
No streaks, ever

Missed days are days. The word "streak" appears nowhere in the product, nor any count of what you missed.

Two rooms
White is shared, black is John

Colour carries the privacy model, so it is felt before it is read. Black surfaces are never seen by the cohort.

The loop Thirty seconds, once a day
01 Email lands At your chosen hour, your local time.
02 You sit The product is closed. Nothing is timed or tracked.
03 One tap From the email or the page. Both land on the same square.
04 The others arrive A beat alone, then the grid fades up beneath you.
05 You leave No feed to scroll, no reason to stay. That is the design.

Part four What a builder needs Tokens, motion, rules, states
Tokens
Paper#F3F1E9 · page
Card#FDFCF8 · surfaces
Warm#FBF4EA · today, tap
Ink#171916 · text, John
Amber#FFAD54 · you, today
Second#5a5c58 · body
Quiet#9a9c97 · labels
Hairlineink @ 14% · 1px
Type

Host Grotesk throughout. Headings 800, uppercase, tracking −.03em. Body 400/500 at 1.6. Labels 700, .56–.62rem, tracking .16em, uppercase. Nothing italic except a principle name.

Space & shape

4px steps. Screen padding 24px mobile / 32px desktop. No border radius anywhere. No shadows — depth is hairlines and colour only. Amber focus rings are the one glow.

Targets

Tap square 240px mobile / 272px desktop. Buttons full-width, 48px minimum. Dots are 6–9px but never interactive; the whole day column is the hit area.

Breakpoint

One, at 48rem. Below: single column, tabs for week / all days. Above: side-by-side, no tabs, content capped at 64rem and centred.

Motion · the anatomy of one tap
0 ms Press

Square dips to 98%, 90ms. One soft haptic. Nothing else moves.

120 ms Amber lands

Fill crosses to amber, "You practised." set inside it. Committed, offline or not.

400–1400 ms Alone

One second of your own day. The count of others fades in at 700ms.

1400 ms The others

Grid fades up over 600ms, dots in day order, 40ms apart. No bounce.

2000 ms Note offered

Rises from the bottom, 300ms. Dismiss and it doesn't come back today.

Everything eases out, nothing eases in and out. No spring, no scale-up, no confetti. Under prefers-reduced-motion all of it becomes a 200ms crossfade and the timings collapse to zero.

States the build must handle
Situation
What happens
Already logged today
Opening goes straight to the cohort view. The tap square is gone, not disabled — nothing to press twice.
Midnight, local
The day rolls in your own timezone. A sit at 1am counts for the night you were awake — yesterday remains addable until the following midnight.
Cohort of six, not ten
Dot columns render the actual cohort size; layout is unaffected. Copy says "five others", never "5/10".
Someone leaves mid-run
Their past marks and notes stay; their column simply stops. Nobody is told, and no gap is labelled.
Nobody has practised yet today
"You're the first one in." Never "0 of 10".
A note is unkind
John can remove any note; the person's mark stays. No reporting UI in Phase 1 — ten people who paid, one host who reads everything.
Link opened on someone else's phone
The magic link is the session; it's long-lived and revocable from Settings. No passwords, ever.
After day 35
After the closing gathering on Wednesday 21 October the log freezes and stays readable at the same link. No emails, no new taps, no upsell on the page.
Words we don't use
Streak · day 6 in a row

Nothing is counted forward, so nothing can be lost.

You missed · don't break it

Absence is never addressed, in app or email.

6/10 · 60% · average

Counts of people, in words. No denominators, no percentages.

Session · minutes · progress

A sit isn’t timed and goes nowhere. We say "practised".

Community · members · users

"The others", "the nine others", names on notes.

Well done · congratulations

The product states what happened. It doesn't praise.

The phrase we do use is compassionate flexibility: twenty minutes is standard, and any honest practice counts. Say the standard first and the flexibility second — never the flexibility alone, or the standard quietly disappears.

Phase 1 build surface
Data

Four tables: person, day-mark, note, private message. A mark is a person, a date, a timestamp. Nothing else is stored about a day.

Send

One scheduled daily send per timezone bucket, one Wednesday letter, transactional replies from John. Every email carries a one-tap log link.

Client

One page, local-first write, queue and sync. No realtime — the grid is fresh on load, and that's honest.

John's side

A private inbox: questions, voice replies, quiet-day flags, note removal. Not designed here — a plain list is enough for ten people.

Everything in this document is Phase 1 except John's inbox. Partners, cohorts beyond ten, and anything social beyond a note are deliberately out.
