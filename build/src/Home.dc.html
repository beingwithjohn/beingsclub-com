What is Beings Club?

Beings Club is where curiosity connects — a space for meditation and conversation.

About Beings Club

Every month, beings gather.

Our monthly online meeting is called a Salon and it starts with meditation. Following, members meet one-to-one and in groups of three. There are no prescribed conversation topics. There is nothing to do except stay curious.

how a salon unfoldsThe form stays reliable so what happens inside it can be free.
01We sit togetherAround twenty minutes of guided meditation practice — individual practice in a shared field. Nothing has to be believed: instructions are offered as an invitation, to experiment with and discover the reality of for yourself.
02Three invitationsBefore the rooms open, three invitations:
1. To feel confident about sharing pronouns and/or correcting name pronunciation in real time, so that you are addressed how you prefer.2. To arrive together without pressure to speak on entering each conversation room.3. To stay curious.
03Curiosity connectsThe first rooms open for thirty minutes as randomly assorted pairs. A two-minute countdown appears when the time is nearly up.
04Again, in threesThe rooms open a second time as randomly assorted triads. Allocation is random, so you may find yourself with the same person... 
05We close togetherEveryone comes back into one room to land, share, and digest together.
The things worth caring about make themselves known.
frequently asked questions
How does membership work?Membership is free and offered after an introductory conversation and a mutual yes.
Is Beings Club only online or in-person too?Monthly salons are online. In-person events happen more rarely.
Are Salons recorded?Nope. Salons exist for those who show up to them.
Is Beings Club a religious thing?Nope. We're a community of curious people, of multiple kinds of background.
I've never meditated. Is that a problem?Nope. Meditation instruction at Beings Club is accessible to all experience levels and you are not required to adopt any beliefs to practice. For those who want to go deeper, John offers more through Space to Be.
If it's free, how is it sustained?Through generosity. Members are invited to contribute if and when they want to.
What if I can only come sometimes?Live your life! Come when you can.
