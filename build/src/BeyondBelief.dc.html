What it's for

Make friends with experience.

Trusting yourself isn't confidence that things will always go perfectly, and it isn't certainty about who you are. It's closer to a friendship — with yourself and life.

Sitting is a way to turn towards our experience. Attention can be placed, and when it wanders, you can return it — gently, precisely. Done often enough, this re-oxygenates the atmosphere of your experience with the qualities of friendship: gentleness, precision, and humour.

Beyond Belief is a guided exploration into your direct experience. You're invited to come along and see what you discover — perhaps it'll be a new kind of friendship.

Enough time for it to become yours.

Each meeting is another chance to explore meditation practice with others, and the days that follow are where it becomes yours.

The shape of it

Five ways of approaching, in an order that matters.

These aren't five topics. They're how a friendship gets built — one quality at a time, each week making the next one possible.

01 Curiosity 16 Sep Opening toward what's actually here, and discovering it.
02 Care 23 Sep Gentleness and warmth — turned toward yourself first, then outwards.
03 Responsibility 30 Sep The ability to respond to what arises — and meet need.
04 Play 7 Oct Giving yourself the generosity of "no wrong way to do it".
05 Rest 14 Oct Pausing every striving, and recognising space uncontrived, and open.
06 The closing 21 Oct We look back over the thirty-five days together, and close.
