About

Beings Club is a realisationhouse for the curious.

We gather as the curious to realise what is possible — in ourselves, between us, and in the world.

Together we share two orienting principles. First: we hold each other as precious. Second: we stay curious.

Our offerings are presented for you to experiment with and discover the reality of for yourself. We ask each participant to take responsibility as a form of care — for ourselves, each other, and the world.

Joining takes a conversation and a mutual yes. After that, you'll receive invitations to our Salons, Sits, and happenings in person.

Story

In the lineage of a tea house.

Beings Club exists because of a tea house at Plum Village, where I found myself in conversations over tea that expanded my sense of what is possible.

The retreat schedule was valuable, but I was drawn again and again to that little tea house and the conversations that could happen there — free of any prescribed topic.

Beings Club is a realisationhouse in the lineage of that atmosphere: a lineage of feeling, not a body of doctrine, a space for conversations of all kinds.

— John

Beyond Belief: The Art of Trusting Yourself

A way into a meditation practice, a deeper sense of trust, and a more embodied life. Each week takes up one of five principles — curiosity, care, responsibility, play, and rest — as lenses to sit through, not ideas to believe. We meet live on Wednesday evenings, and you're paired with a different fellow sitter each week.

Not a course you attend — a 35-day experiment. We sit every day we can, each in our own room and our own hour, knowing nine others are sitting the same days with the same principle. No required length; five minutes on a hard day is a real sit, not a failed one. Missed days are expected — beginning again is the practice.

What makes Beings Club possible.

01

We hold each other as precious

Each life in the room, including the ones nothing like yours, is treated as real and valuable and worth our care.

This posture does not ask that you erase yourself. It asks you to extend care in every direction. Your own life is precious on the same terms.

This is held sacred, and it is why everything else is free to change. It frames how we meet each other and how we meet ourselves.

We are beings, together.

02

Curiosity connects

We define curiosity as “an orientation to experience that is open to discovery”.

Practised in this way, we create the conditions for the discovery of connection — to ourselves, to each other, and to what is possible.

We can meet whatever arises with curiosity, inclusive of any conflict or tension. We can even be curious about whether we should be curious at all.

Stay curious.

The moment you stop treating the edge of the possible as fixed, reality reveals its true nature.

Vision

A micro-culture of creative realisation, in service of life's flourishing.

Creativity is only ever limited by our sense of what's possible — and that sense is not fixed. It grows, or it shrinks, depending on the company we keep.

Beings Club is a space built to let it grow. When people hold each other as precious and stay curious, the edge of what feels possible starts to move — and once it moves, we find new ways to reach toward it. That's how the future arrives: not waiting ahead of us, but showing up wherever people make room for it.

We hope this won't be the only such room. We imagine realisationhouses becoming ordinary and widespread, each with its own atmosphere and its own way of gathering — enough of them to change what a culture believes it can do.

Curiosity is the ground it all rests on, and it asks to be practised. Sitting is simply curiosity turned inward — the same attention we bring to each other, offered to ourselves. Not something to believe in; something to play with and discover the reality of for yourself.

What's at stake

Whether we can share a planet without tearing it, or each other, apart — and find some joy in the company while we do.

What unfolds is unplanned, ordinary, and sometimes magic.

A note from John

We do this together.

I'll be honest about why Beings Club exists.

There's a more beautiful world our hearts know is possible. And to realise it, we're asked to be the ones who can live in it.

It isn't a thing that one person can build and others will visit. It only happens as we live it together — which means it only happens if you're part of it.

Beings Club is held and designed to be a place where we can live it together. Salons are for meeting each other with genuine curiosity and care across difference. Sits are for deepening our own capacity to meet every moment with heart and strength.

So this isn't an invitation in the usual sense. I'm not offering you something finished. I'm inviting you into something that doesn't exist without you, and asking you to help make it real.

We do this together, or it doesn't happen.

— John
