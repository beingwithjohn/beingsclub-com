Welcome, being.

Beings Club is where curiosity connects.

Curiosity connects.

At Beings Club, we define curiosity as an orientation to experience that is open to discovery. Curiosity connects us to ourselves, each other and what is possible.

The Salon is the heart of it.

The Salon is a monthly online space for practice and conversation. We begin with a guided curiosity practice, then meet one-to-one and in groups of three. There are no prompts or themes—only an invitation to stay curious.

the principles

A few things before we begin.

Beings Club is made by the people who participate. Here’s all we ask of you, in plain terms:

Bring your curiosity. Curiosity is an orientation to experience that is open to discovery. Bring that openness to yourself, other people and what happens here.

Keep people’s confidence. What someone shares in a Salon stays there. You can share your own experience afterwards, but not another person’s story or identity without their permission.

Let connection stay free. You may invite a conversation to continue beyond a Salon. Nobody owes their availability; if you cannot or do not want to meet, respond clearly and kindly.

You can leave at any time.

The next Salon will appear here when it is announced.

What the Salons leave behind.

Thoughts, questions, images and references members chose to share. Field Notes are not a conversation: there are no responses, reactions or comments.

an invitation from the Salon

What stayed with you?

You’re invited to share something of what you discovered at the Salon this month: a thought, question, image, reference or anything else from the Salon. Please respect the privacy and confidentiality of your conversations and remember that members who were not at the Salon will also be able to see what you share.
