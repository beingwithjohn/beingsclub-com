Welcome, being.

Beings Club is where curiosity connects.

Curiosity connects.

At Beings Club, we define curiosity as an orientation to experience that is open to discovery. Curiosity connects us to ourselves, to each other, and to what is possible.

The Salon is the heart of it.

The Salon is a monthly online space for practice and conversation. We begin with a guided curiosity practice, then meet one-to-one and in groups of three. There are no prompts or themes. There is only an invitation to stay curious.

the principles

A few things before we begin.

Beings Club is made by those who participate. Here’s all we ask of you, in plain terms:

Bring your curiosity. Curiosity is an orientation to experience that is open to discovery. Bring that openness to yourself, other people and what happens here.

Keep people’s confidence. What someone shares in a Salon stays there. You can share your own experience afterwards, but not another person’s story or identity without their permission.

Let connection stay free. You may invite a conversation to continue beyond a Salon. Nobody owes their availability; if you cannot or do not want to meet, respond clearly and kindly.

You can leave at any time.

field notes

What members choose to share.

Thoughts, questions, images and references from people who join the Salons. Field Notes are not a conversation. There are no responses, reactions or comments.

an invitation from the Salon

What stayed with you?

You’re invited to share something of what you discovered at this month’s Salon: a thought, question, image, reference or anything else that stayed with you. Please respect the privacy and confidentiality of your conversations. Members who were not at the Salon will also be able to see what you share.
